About Us

**Apport Software Solutions Private Limited incorporated in Feb 2021** with a vision to develop a comprehensive end-to-end mobile commerce suite that provides all the necessary tools to small businesses to grow, engage, retain, and monetize their customer base effectively on mobile.

QuickSell App started in 2017 as a mobile cataloguing app to enable small B2B customers (think jewellery and clothing wholesalers) to showcase their products more effectively over WhatsApp. B2B merchants use WhatsApp effectively to showcase 1000s of designs to customers almost every week.

Today, over 6 Lakh+ stores are created on our platform in 100+ countries, purely organically. The product design's simplicity and ease of use have enabled the viral adoption of the QuickSell App globally.

As COVID has spread worldwide, we are seeing even greater adoption than before. As a result, more and more SMBs are actively looking for ways to digitize their business and sell virtually.
